<p align="center">
  Need help with a logo? A distro missing from the catalog, or broken art?

<h2 id="get-help" align="center">Get Help</h2>

<ul>
  <li><b>Issues:</b> <a href="https://github.com/xfetch-cli/logos/issues">github.com/xfetch-cli/logos/issues</a></li>
  <li><b>Docs:</b> <a href="./README.md">catalog README</a>, <a href="./CONTRIBUTING.md">contributing guide</a></li>
  <li><b>Core usage:</b> <code>xfetch --gen-config --logo &lt;id&gt;</code> (see the xfetch docs)</li>
  <li><b>Security reports:</b> <a href="mailto:x@xscriptor.com">x@xscriptor.com</a> (private disclosure)</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="before-opening-an-issue" align="center">Before Opening an Issue</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Check the <a href="./logos.json">index</a> — the logo may exist under a different id or alias.</li>
  <li>For a missing distro, include the <code>ID</code> and <code>ID_LIKE</code> from <code>/etc/os-release</code>.</li>
  <li>For broken art, name the file and include a screenshot of the rendered output.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="response-expectations" align="center">Response Expectations</h2>

<p>
  Maintainers triage reports based on impact and reproducibility. Clear, minimal reproductions are resolved faster.
</p>
